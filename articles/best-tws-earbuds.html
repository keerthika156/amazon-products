Best TWS Earbuds for Everyday Use

True Wireless Stereo (TWS) earbuds are convenient for listening to music, watching videos, attending online classes and making calls without dealing with cables.

There are many TWS earbuds available at different prices. Before buying a pair, consider battery life, comfort, microphone quality, connectivity and the features that you actually need.

What should you look for in TWS earbuds?

  • Comfortable fit
  • Good battery life
  • Reliable Bluetooth connectivity
  • Clear microphone for calls
  • Suitable audio quality
  • Charging case
  • Water or sweat resistance if required

How to choose the right TWS earbuds

If you mainly use earbuds for online classes and calls, microphone quality and comfort should be important factors.

If you frequently travel, consider battery life and the charging case. For workouts, look for a suitable level of water or sweat resistance.

Things to check before buying

  1. Check the exact model number.
  2. Compare battery specifications.
  3. Check whether your phone supports the required Bluetooth features.
  4. Look at the manufacturer's warranty information.
  5. Check the current Amazon price before purchasing.

Final thoughts

The best TWS earbuds depend on your individual requirements. A budget model may be sufficient for casual listening, while another user may prefer features such as active noise cancellation, longer battery life or improved call quality.

Compare the features and specifications of the exact model before making your purchase.
